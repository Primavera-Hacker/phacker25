We invite those who work, research, create, or sabotage technologies to submit proposals. We want to open a space for narratives that unsettle, dislocate, or move us. We are looking for ideas, tools, narratives, gestures, or failures that allow us to understand and subvert the architecture of the present digital world.

## **Thematic Axes**

Primavera Hacker organizes its content into curatorial axes. When submitting your proposal, choose the one that best relates to your topic:

### **Art and Computational Aesthetics**

We seek works, actions, or reflections that use technologies to alter sensibilities, produce dissonance, and expand language. Installations, glitches, texts, demos; strictly no conceptual performances.

### **Politics, Ethics, and Accelerationism**

The network is reality. What do we do when digital infrastructure becomes a device for affective and political control? We want narratives that address legislation, surveillance, platforms, borders, affects, attention capitalism, and digital disobedience.

### **Hardware, Tactics, and Materiality**

Everything that grounds the digital world in the physical world. Device hacks, open-source boards, recycled machines, modified sensors. We are interested in both design and repair.

### **Science and Knowledge**

We share a deep distrust for the savior narratives of tech-solutionism. This axis seeks formal and informal research that provides critical frameworks for thinking about the crisis. We also find it important to stop demonizing scientific materialism. That not all knowledge is scientific does not mean that scientific knowledge is unnecessary, nor that we cannot use it strategically.

### **Security, Self-Defense, and Cyber Offense**

Talks that explore digital security from multiple fronts: from the offensive analysis of protocols, binaries, applications, or embedded systems, to tools and methodologies linked to operations, threat intelligence, or frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK. We also open space for proposals focused on encryption, privacy, pedagogies of digital self-defense, and technologies designed to protect at-risk communities. We are interested in both exploitation-oriented research and the construction of collective capabilities to resist and survive in hostile environments.

## **How to Participate**

Submit your proposal through our content management system:
👉 https://phacker.org/cfp _(available soon)_

Talks can last **30 or 45 minutes** (plus Q&A). If you wish to present something non-conventional (installation, workshop, intervention, etc.), or need more time, please indicate this clearly.

## **Submission Guidelines**

Please include:

- A clear and accessible title
- A summary of your talk or proposal
- A short biography, reference links, and your motivation
- The estimated time you need

Avoid cryptic names or inside jokes. We want to understand what your proposal is about without needing to know you beforehand.

We do not accept promotional or commercial presentations.

## **Travel Support**

Primavera Hacker is a non-profit event. There is no payment for presenting, but if you are selected you can request partial support for your travel or an invitation letter for visa procedures.
